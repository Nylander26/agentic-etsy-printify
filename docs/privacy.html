Privacy Policy

Application: LLD Studio Automation · Operated by LLD
Effective date: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LLD ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our internal automation application ("the App"). The App is a single-organization tool used to manage our own print-on-demand brand presence. It is not a public, multi-user product, and we do not offer services to third-party end users.

1. Scope

The App connects only to accounts and services that we own and operate. It accesses the Pinterest account belonging to our business via the official Pinterest API, using credentials we authorize ourselves. We do not access, collect, or process data belonging to other Pinterest users.

2. Information We Access

Through the Pinterest API, the App may access:

  • Basic profile/account information for our own business account (account ID, username).
  • Our own boards and Pins (to create, schedule, and manage Pins).
  • Aggregate engagement and analytics for our own Pins and account (e.g. impressions, saves, clicks) used to inform our product and content decisions.

The App also works with content we own outside Pinterest — our product designs and the corresponding listing URLs on our own e-commerce storefront — in order to publish Pins that link back to those listings.

3. How We Use Information

  • To publish and schedule Pins of our own product designs.
  • To link those Pins to our own product listings.
  • To read aggregate performance metrics for our own account to guide our research and marketing decisions.

We do not use this information for advertising to third parties, profiling, or resale.

4. Data Storage and Retention

The App runs in our own private environment. Authentication tokens and operational data are stored locally and are used solely to operate the App. We retain data only as long as needed for these purposes and delete it when no longer required. We do not maintain a public database of Pinterest data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following services strictly to operate our own brand workflow:

  • Pinterest API — to publish Pins and read our own account analytics.
  • Printify / Etsy — our print-on-demand and storefront providers for our own listings.
  • Google Gemini — to generate design and metadata content.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. We share only what is necessary to operate our own accounts on those platforms.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share Pinterest data with third parties. Information is used only for the internal operation of our own brand described above.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect access credentials and data, including keeping API tokens out of version control and limiting access to authorized operators of our organization.

8. Revoking Access

Because the App accesses only our own Pinterest account, access can be revoked at any time from our Pinterest account settings, which immediately stops the App's access to the Pinterest API.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is an internal business tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
